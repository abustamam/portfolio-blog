```text
Situation: I am a ________ and ________________________.
           (who you are, what's going on, who it's for)

Task:      Please ________________________________.
           (the ONE thing you want done)

Input:     ________________________________
           (paste it below, made safe: PERSON-1, EMAIL-1, no account numbers)

Format:    ________________________________
           (length, tone, layout: "a table with columns...", "under 100 words", "bullet points")

Check:     Before you answer, ________________________________.
           (what it should flag, verify or admit it had to guess)
```

## Worked example

> **Situation:** I run a monthly food drive with 12 volunteers. Most are retirees who read email on their phones.
> **Task:** Draft the reminder for Saturday's shift.
> **Input:** Shifts are 8-10am (PERSON-1, PERSON-2, PERSON-3) and 10am-12pm (PERSON-4, PERSON-5). We meet at the side entrance.
> **Format:** Under 80 words, friendly, with one line per shift. No emojis.
> **Check:** List anything you had to guess, and don't add details I didn't give you.

## Good "Check" lines to borrow

- List anything you had to guess or assume.
- Tell me where each fact came from. If you aren't sure, say so.
- Don't do the arithmetic yourself. Give me the spreadsheet formula instead.
- Ask me up to three questions first if anything is unclear.
- Point out anything in my input that looks wrong or inconsistent.

## Your task in one sentence (session 1)

> Every week I do **________** using **________**, it takes **________**, and done means **________**.

**By track**
- **Everyday AI:** fill in the template as it is. If your task uses a file, describe its real shape in *Input*: the column names, the document type, how many rows.
- **Connect:** pick a task that starts in your email or calendar. In *Input*, say which threads or meeting requests it starts from. In *Check*, say what you approve before anything is sent.
- **Build:** under the template, list the steps an agent would take: where the input comes from, what the AI does, where it stops for your approval, and where the result goes. You'll use that list in session 3.
