## Five weekly tasks

Each card is one task that comes round every week in that job. The Before and After buttons show how the week goes without AI and with it. Try this prompt puts that card's prompt into the builder further down.

## The prompt in five parts

The prompt from the card you picked shows up here, split into its five parts. Change any part to fit your own week and the full prompt underneath changes with it. Copy it into whichever AI app you use ([Which AI app should I use?](/ai-guides/which-tool/) helps if you haven't picked one), then check the answer the way the card says.
