## Before you paste anything

A résumé is mostly about you, and what you share about yourself is your call. Three things should come out before any of it goes into an AI chat:

- **Your references.** Their names, phone numbers and emails. They agreed to take a call from an employer, not to be pasted into an AI tool. Leave them off completely.
- **Your current employer's data.** Client names, internal figures, documents from work. Describe what you did ("kept the client list up to date") without pasting the list.
- **Your full address, phone and email.** Delete the contact block at the top of your résumé before you paste it, and add it back at the end. The tool does not need it to do the work.

The same rules apply to everything else you paste. See [what never to paste into an AI chat](/ai-guides/never-paste/).

## 1. Tailor your résumé to one posting

Paste the posting and your résumé, and ask the tool to reorder and reword what is already there. The important part is the last line: it asks the tool to list anything it added. AI tools sometimes add a skill or a number you never gave them, and it reads just as confidently as the true parts.

When the list comes back, check it, then read the new résumé side by side with your original anyway. The list can miss things. Anything you cannot back up in an interview comes out.

## 2. A cover letter that doesn't read as AI

A cover letter reads as AI when the tool writes the content. So give it the content: why you want this job, what you bring, and anything you want to explain, in your own rough words. The tool's job is only the order and the structure.

<pre><code>{assemblePrompt(COVER_LETTER)}</code></pre>

<p><button type="button" class="ai-link" data-prompt-fill="cover-letter" data-prompt-target="job-search-builder">Put this in the prompt builder ↓</button></p>

Then cut:

- Any sentence you would not say out loud to the person interviewing you.
- Any claim that is not in your notes.
- Stock openings and closings, and big words you never use.
- Lines that repeat your résumé. The letter is for the why; the résumé already has the what.

Read what is left out loud. If it does not sound like you, change the words until it does.

## 3. Practise the interview

One prompt turns the tool into an interviewer. It asks a question, waits for your answer, then asks a follow-up, the way a real interviewer digs into what you said. Answer the way you would in the room, not the way you would write it.

<pre><code>{assemblePrompt(INTERVIEW_PRACTICE)}</code></pre>

<p><button type="button" class="ai-link" data-prompt-fill="interview-practice" data-prompt-target="job-search-builder">Put this in the prompt builder ↓</button></p>

The feedback is a starting point, not a verdict. The tool does not know the clinic or the person who will interview you.
