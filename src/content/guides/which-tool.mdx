## Where to start

If it is for work, use what your employer allows, signed in with your work account.

If it is for you, start with the free <Term id="chat-app">app</Term> that matches your email: Gemini if you use Gmail, Copilot if you use Outlook. If you use neither, ChatGPT or Claude.

Stay on the free plan until you hit its <Term id="usage-limit">limits</Term> every week, or until you want the <Term>AI</Term> inside Gmail, Word or Outlook.

## What I'd pick, by situation

### Work, with a Microsoft account

Use what your employer allows. That is most likely Copilot Chat, signed in with your work account, not a personal one. Don't copy work files into a personal AI account, even one you pay for.

### Work, with a Google account

Same rule: ask what is allowed. If your company has Gemini turned on, use it with your work account.

### Home, if your household runs on Gmail

I'd start with Gemini on your personal Google Account. It is free with your Google Account, and it can connect to your Gmail, Calendar and Drive once you turn that on in Connected Apps.

### Looking for a job

Use a personal account, never your current employer's. Any of the four free plans can help you rewrite a résumé or practice interview answers. Check in the table whether your chats are used to train the AI, and leave out other people's details (see [what's safe to paste into AI](/ai-guides/never-paste/)). [AI for your job search](/ai-guides/job-search/) walks through one application from start to finish.

### Writing code

You can ask any of them about code in an ordinary chat. If you have no account yet, ChatGPT's free plan includes limited access to its coding tool.

## Free or paid?

For learning the habit, the free plan of any of these is enough. The usual personal plan is about $20 a month, and Google and Microsoft also sell cheaper ones; exact prices are in the table. Every company has much bigger plans too, at around $100 a month and up, which everyday work doesn't need. I would pay when one of these is true:

- You hit [the limit](/ai-guides/usage-limits/) most weeks and have to wait or stop.
- You want it inside the apps you already use. Gemini inside Gmail and Docs, and Copilot inside Word, Excel and Outlook, come with the paid personal plans.
- You need longer files or more <Term id="attach">uploads</Term> than the free plan allows.

A bigger plan won't rescue a vague request. When the free plan gives you a poor answer, ask more clearly before you think about upgrading, and check what comes back either way.
