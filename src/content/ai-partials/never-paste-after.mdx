## Check before you press Enter

On an account that isn't approved for this kind of data, ask yourself:

- [ ] Could anyone in this text be recognized by someone who knows them?
- [ ] Is there any number that unlocks money or an account?
- [ ] Would I be comfortable if this text were shown to the people it's about?

## If you already pasted something

If it was a password or key, **change it now**, whichever account it went into. If it went into an account that isn't approved for it: delete the conversation, and look in the tool's settings for the switch that stops your chats being used for <Term>training</Term>. If it was work or client information, tell whoever handles that at work. Deleting the chat doesn't un-send it.
