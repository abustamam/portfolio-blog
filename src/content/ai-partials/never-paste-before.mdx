## First: which account?

What you can safely paste depends on the account you are signed in to. Check that before anything else.

- **A free or personal account, or a work file in a personal account:** treat the <Term>chat</Term> like an email to a stranger. Keep the list below out of it, or make the file safe first. Your employer may not allow work files in personal accounts at all; their rule comes first.
- **A work account your employer has approved for that kind of data:** follow their policy. You may not need to remove anything at all. Paste what they allow, and nothing they don't. Your employer's agreement with the <Term>AI</Term> company, and their policy, decide what's allowed.
- **Not sure which you have:** treat it as the first case until you've asked.

Passwords, keys and one-time codes stay out of every account. That includes a work account your employer has approved.

## What to keep out of an account that isn't approved for it

On a free or personal account, or any account your employer hasn't approved for this kind of data:

| Never paste | Do this instead |
|---|---|
| **Other people's personal details**: names, emails, phone numbers, addresses | Swap in placeholders: PERSON-1, EMAIL-1, PHONE-1. Keep the key (who is who) in your own file. |
| **Passwords, API keys, one-time codes** | Never, not even "just to check it". Describe the problem without the secret. |
| **Account and card numbers** | Mask them: \*\*\*\*4821, or ACCOUNT-1. |
| **Health details** about you or anyone else | Generalize: "a family member with a chronic condition". |

## Make a file safe in three steps

1. **Decide what the AI actually needs.** To get formulas, it needs the column headers and three example rows, not the whole sheet. To group errors in a log, it needs the error messages, not the usernames.
2. **Replace, don't delete.** Use the same placeholder for the same thing every time (every appearance of one person becomes PERSON-1). That keeps the patterns, such as "the same person gave 12 times", without the name.
3. **Keep the key yourself.** The list of PERSON-1 = the real name stays in your own sheet and never goes into the chat.

A placeholder hides the name, not the person. If what's left could only be one person (a rare job title, a small town, an unusual amount), take that out too.

### Tricks that help
- *Spreadsheets:* add an ID column, then make a copy with the name and email columns deleted. <Term>Upload</Term> the copy.
- *Text:* Find and replace (Ctrl+H, or Cmd+Shift+H on a Mac in Google Docs and Sheets) each name with a placeholder before you copy anything.
- *Ask for the method, not the answer:* "Write me a formula that..." means your data never has to leave the sheet.
